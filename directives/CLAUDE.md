# directives/CLAUDE.md

Local conventions for this subtree. Read the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) first.

## Rules

- SOPs and runbooks only — step-by-step, human-readable. No business logic or code lives here (root CLAUDE.md: "No business logic in directives").
- Every directive defines: goals, inputs, outputs, edge cases, safety constraints, and how success is verified.
- Directives are living documents — update them when the process they describe changes, not after the fact.
- Validated for: required sections present, referenced files/scripts actually exist, markdown integrity, and no step whose only source is an undocumented conversation, Slack thread, or person's name.
